Water Well Mechanics

Initial State:

Once placed on the grid, the well starts empty.

NPCs cannot collect water while it’s empty.

Filling and Generation:

Show a chat message when the well is full.

Each well generates 5 buckets of water, at a rate of 1 bucket every 3 s.

Farmer Duties

Task Queue

Must finish the current task before starting a new one.

If there are multiple farmer houses on the grid, each farmer “remembers” their home and won’t duplicate another’s work.

Overall Flow

Prepare soil

Collect water & irrigate

Wait for planting

Manage plant growth

Harvest & store

Return home (if no tasks remain)

1. Prepare Soil
Trigger: placing a planting plot shows the 🚧 emoji on that tile.

Routine:

Farmer leaves home and walks to the tile.

Prepares soil for 5 s, alternating icons:

🔨 (1 s) → 🪚 (1 s) → 🔨 … until 5 s elapse.

At the end, remove 🚧 and display 🟧 on the plot; farmer returns to 👨🏼‍🌾.

Well Check:

If there’s any well with water in the grid, go to the nearest one; otherwise,

Proceed to the next available task or return home.

2. Collect Water & Irrigate
Collect 1 Bucket:

Walk to the well (5 s); use 🪣 during collection.

After 5 s, revert to 👨🏼‍🌾 carrying 1 bucket.

Irrigation:

Walk to the prepared plot (5 s); emoji 🚿 while irrigating.

When done, plot changes to 🟫; farmer returns to 👨🏼‍🌾.

3. Planting
Wait: stand beside the plot for 10 s or until a seed is selected.

Seed Selection:

Must have seeds available in Storage > Seeds.

Player clicks the plot to choose the seed.

Planting Routine:

Walk to storage and pick 1 seed (10 s); decrement seed stock.

Return to the plot and plant the selected seed (no extra time).

Continue planting that same seed until another is chosen.

If no tasks remain, return home.

4. Plant Growth
Stages:

🌱 — sprout: 25 s after planting

🌿 — young plant: another 25 s

Harvest emoji — changes to the specific seed’s icon when ready

5. Harvest & Storage
Detection: if a plant is ripe, the farmer:

Leaves home (or current location).

Walks to the plant and harvests (5 s); emoji 🧺 during harvest.

Returns to 👨🏼‍🌾 when done.

Storage:

Walk to storage (5 s); emoji 📦 during storing.

Each harvest yields 2–5 items, added under Storage > Harvests.

If no tasks remain, go home.

Emoji Legend
👨🏼‍🌾 — Farmer

🚧 — Plot placed

🔨 / 🪚 — Preparing soil

🟧 — Soil prepared

🪣 — Collecting water

🚿 — Irrigating

🟫 — Soil irrigated

🌱 — Seed planted (sprout)

🌿 — Young plant

[Seed’s Emoji] — Plant ready for harvest

🧺 — Harvesting

📦 — Storing produce
